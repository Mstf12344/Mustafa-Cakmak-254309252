Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This page explains what information World Kitchen (dunya-mutfagi.vercel.app) uses and why.

What information is stored?

  • Accounts created with a username: your username, a hash of your password (the password itself is never stored), favourites, notes and settings stay only in your own browser (localStorage) and are never sent to a server.
  • Accounts created with e-mail or Google: your e-mail address and display name (for Google sign-in, the name and e-mail Google shares), together with your favourites, notes, ticked ingredients and site settings, are stored with Supabase (server location: Frankfurt, Germany) so you can use your account on any device.

What is it used for?

Only to sign you in and to show you the recipe data you saved. When you create an account with e-mail or Google, the site may send you a single welcome e-mail. Your information is not used for advertising, is not sold and is not shared with third parties.

Google sign-in

When you sign in with Google, the site receives only your name, e-mail address and profile ID from Google. It has no access to anything else in your Google account.

Third-party content

Recipe photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons and fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to those services when you view the site. Video buttons open a search on YouTube in a new tab. These services have their own privacy policies.

Security

All connections are encrypted with HTTPS. Database rules allow each user to see and change only their own record.

Deleting your data

You can delete your saved data with “Delete profile” in the profile menu. To have your account removed completely, open an issue on the project’s GitHub page.

Cookies

The site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Your session and settings are kept in your browser’s local storage.

Visit statistics

We count page views with Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and does not identify you personally; it records anonymous information such as the page visited, country, device type and browser.
